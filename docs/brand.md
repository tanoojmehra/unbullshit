# Brand

**Wordmark:** `unbullshit.` (lowercase, closing period)

**Tagline:** Build what matters. Cut what doesn't.

**Visual design:** Charcoal `#171717`, amber `#F5B83D`, warm white `#F4F4F0`. The amber U crossed by a white diagonal line symbolizes cutting unnecessary work while preserving the useful core.

The repository includes original, scalable [symbol](../assets/logo.svg) and [wordmark](../assets/wordmark.svg). No paid fonts or external assets are needed.

**Voice:** clear, practical, evidence-led, willing to disagree without attacking the person.

**Supporting language:** "Think before building. Investigate before criticizing. Improve without ceremony."

Avoid claiming benchmarked savings, certified safety, or universal agent support without evidence. This branding doesn't assert trademark registration.
